About DJ's Mobile Rust & Mechanical

We've been doing this for over 20 years.

Not just servicing cars — dealing with rust, doing diagnostics, fitting brakes, carrying out roadworthies, coding keys. The full scope of what a vehicle actually needs. That experience doesn't come from a manual. It comes from doing the work, day after day, on all kinds of vehicles. See all our services.

DJ's Mobile Rust & Mechanical is a family business. We're based in Logan, and we work across the area — Springwood, Beenleigh, Browns Plains, Loganholme, and the suburbs around them. We know the roads here. We know the people here.

What makes us different from most mechanics is simple: we come to you. You don't have to drop your car off, organise a lift, or take half a day out of your schedule. We show up at your home, your workplace, wherever the car is — and we do the job there.

The other thing that sets us apart is what we cover. Most mobile mechanics won't touch rust. Most rust specialists aren't mechanics. We do both. So if your car needs a rust assessment on top of a service, or a roadworthy along with some brake work, you're not ringing around trying to find three different people. You're ringing us.

We're not the biggest operation. We don't have a fancy shopfront. But we know our trade, we're straight with our customers, and we stand behind the work we do. That's what's kept us going for more than two decades.

If you've got a question about your car, give us a call. We're happy to chat.
